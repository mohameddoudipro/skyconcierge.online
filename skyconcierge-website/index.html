Compliance

Short-let rules in London

Before you list, check the rules that apply to your property. We help every owner stay compliant from day one.

General information only, not legal or tax advice.

  • 90-night limit Entire homes in Greater London can be let short-term for up to 90 nights per calendar year (Deregulation Act 2015). Going beyond this needs planning permission from your borough.
  • Lease and building rules Check your lease, mortgage and freeholder terms, as some restrict short lets.
  • Safety Working smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, gas safety and electrical checks, fire safety information and suitable host insurance.
  • Tax and records Declare rental income to HMRC and keep clear records of income and expenses.

How do I get paid?

You receive a monthly statement showing every booking, fee and cost, followed by a bank transfer of your earnings.

Why use a short-let management company?

Hosting well takes daily work: messages at all hours, cleaning between stays, pricing and repairs. We handle all of it so you earn from your property without the time commitment.

Which areas of London do you cover?

We manage properties across Greater London. Contact us with your postcode and we will confirm availability.

How much does your service cost?

Our fee is a percentage of booking revenue, depending on the property and the services you choose. We provide a clear quote after your free appraisal.

What about the 90-night rule in London?

Entire homes in Greater London can be let on a short-term basis for up to 90 nights per calendar year without planning permission. We track this for you and can arrange medium-term lets for the rest of the year.

Can I still use my property?

Yes. You can block dates in the calendar for your own stays at any time.

Who handles damage or problems during a stay?

We respond to issues straight away, report any damage to you with photos, coordinate repairs and handle claims with the booking platform on your behalf.
